// What It Does

Tools for the
people on duty

Everything here is built around what a traffic operator actually needs during a shift — not what looks good on a slide deck.

📡
Vehicle Count — Live

Cameras and road sensors count vehicles per lane every 100ms. No manual counting, no guesswork — the system always knows what's on the road.

🧠
Auto Phase Timing

Instead of fixed timers, the system looks at actual traffic on each side and gives green time where it's needed most — automatically.

🟢
Green Wave on Main Roads

On busy corridors, signals coordinate with each other so vehicles don't hit red lights back to back. Smooth flow from one end to the other.

🚨
Emergency Vehicle Priority

When an ambulance or fire truck is approaching, the system clears the path immediately. No waiting, no manual override needed from the control room.

📊
Control Room Dashboard

See every intersection at once. Queue lengths, signal status, alerts — and override any signal manually if the situation on ground needs it.

⚙️
Works Without Internet

The AI runs on hardware installed at each junction. If the network goes down, signals keep working. No dependency on a remote server.

// Under the Hood

From sensor
to signal — 50ms

Four steps happen every time the system makes a decision. It runs continuously, all day, at every intersection.

01 — SENSE
Read the Road

Road sensors and cameras count how many vehicles are waiting in each lane, how fast traffic is moving, and how long the queue has been building.

02 — ANALYZE
Understand the Load

The system builds a picture of the full intersection — which direction is backed up, which side is moving, where the pressure is coming from.

03 — DECIDE
Pick the Best Phase

Based on what it sees, the AI picks how long to keep the current signal and when to switch — always trying to clear the most vehicles with the least waiting.

04 — ACT
Update the Signals

The signal changes. Nearby intersections are notified so they can adjust too — keeping traffic moving as a connected network, not just one junction at a time.
